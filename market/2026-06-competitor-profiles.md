# Competitor Profiles

Source table: `market/2026-06-competitor-ledger.csv`.

## Short Findings

1. Excel, WordPress, карточки товаров, базы клиентов and Telegram bots are crowded. There are sellers with hundreds and thousands of reviews.
2. The opening is not "I also do Excel" or "I also make bots". The opening is narrower: business tables with accounting logic, Codex/AI workflows with documents, B2B offer architecture, and clean task decomposition.
3. Legal-adjacent work is less represented in the local scrape, but live Kwork orders show demand for contracts, tenders, certification and disputes.
4. Nastya should not pretend to be a full legal firm. Stronger wording: documents, checks, protocol of disagreements, chronology, calculations, counterparty logic, package for next step.
5. Vlad should not pretend to be a regular dev studio. Stronger wording: "I turn messy business tasks into a working prompt, prototype, table, ТЗ, КП or Codex task."

1. Nastya: бухгалтерский/договорный порядок where tables, documents and business reality meet.
2. Vlad: messy business task to prompt/TZ/prototype/offer/CRM-ready system.
